# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-specified Bun, native ES-module JavaScript, JSDoc, and plain HTML/CSS.
ccusage is the only runtime dependency. No build step or database.

## Users and Purpose

Developers who use several coding agents on one machine. Show historical
tokens and estimated costs from locally retained logs through ccusage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Six summary cards, monthly tool/model stacks, sortable all-time/month tables,
tool filtering, manual refresh, and honest missing-data states. Group dates
in UTC. Calls, reasoning, provider, and precise pricing provenance are unavailable.
Tests use disposable synthetic data only. Bind to 127.0.0.1; no remote assets.

## Brand Commitments

The implementation brief fixes a compact, restrained dark developer tool,
system fonts, readable data, and no marketing, decoration, gradients, or motion.

## Product Principles

- ccusage owns log parsing and pricing.
- Preserve authoritative totals; label unattributed usage.
- Costs are not invoices or subscription charges.
- Keep private local data out of tests, logs, and the public repository.

## Accessibility

Native controls, keyboard tooltips and sorting, clear focus, 375px layout,
200% zoom, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
