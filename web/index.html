    Calls, Reasoning, provider identity, and exact recorded-versus-estimated pricing provenance are not provided by ccusage. A dash means unavailable, not zero.

    Pricing source: ccusage auto: recorded or estimated. Unpriced-only rows show Unavailable; positive partial amounts are known subtotals. No amount here is an invoice or a subscription charge.

    Unattributed usage reconciles exported model breakdowns to authoritative tool totals, including extra tokens ccusage does not classify. It is not reasoning.

    Typical 30d values project lifetime totals over the inclusive span of observed UTC dates. Recent averages use today and the prior 29 UTC dates, divided by active dates (at least one). These differ from precise event-based lifetime averages.
